Club history

Built on passion, sacrifice, and unforgettable nights.

Liverpool FC is more than a team. It is a symbol of resilience, community, and belief — a club that has lifted generations through heartbreak, glory, and the unforgettable roar of Anfield.

1892

Founded in the red city

Liverpool Football Club was founded following a split from Everton and began a journey that would define a city’s identity.

1950s–60s

The golden era of legends

The clubs of Bill Shankly and Bob Paisley turned Liverpool into a force built on grit, unity, and remarkable success in England and Europe.

1970s–80s

European brilliance

Liverpool became a continental giant, capturing European Cups and creating a standard of excellence that still inspires the club today.

Modern era

Champions of the world

The modern Reds continue to compete at the highest level, blending tradition with relentless ambition and world-class talent.
